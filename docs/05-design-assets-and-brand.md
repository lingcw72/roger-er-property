## UI and image standards

- Accessibility: adequate contrast, semantic headings, descriptive alt text and keyboard-accessible controls.
- Images: accurate captions/alt text; no misleading before/after edits; optimise formats and dimensions.
- Avoid stock imagery that could imply the client’s staff, projects or premises unless clearly appropriate and licensed.
