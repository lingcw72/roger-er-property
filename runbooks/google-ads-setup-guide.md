## Prerequisites

- Business owns the Ads account, billing and administrator access.
- Website lead flow, consent and GTM/GA4 tracking have been tested.
- Budget, service area, landing pages, claims, response process and conversion definitions are approved.

## Setup and validation

1. Enable auto-tagging and link the approved GA4 property.
2. Create conversion actions: confirmed form success may be primary; CTA clicks are normally observation/secondary actions until business outcomes can be verified.
3. Configure tags through GTM and test without clicking live ads.
4. Launch only approved campaigns, geographic targeting, keywords, negatives, budgets and ad copy.
5. Record each genuine lead in the CRM/Sheet; where reliable, import qualified-lead/job outcomes as offline conversions.
6. Review search terms, costs, conversion quality and policy notices routinely.
