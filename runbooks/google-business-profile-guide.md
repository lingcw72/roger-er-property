## Before you start

- The business owns the Google account and remains a primary owner.
- Confirm the exact business name, category, service area, phone, website, hours and public-address policy.
- Keep evidence for any verification request; do not create duplicate profiles.

## Setup

1. Sign in with the business-owned account and search Google Maps for an existing profile.
2. Claim the existing accurate profile, or create one if none exists.
3. Enter the real business name and most accurate primary category; add only relevant secondary categories.
4. Choose storefront or service-area business accurately. Hide a residential/non-customer-facing address where appropriate.
5. Add approved phone, website, hours, service areas, description and photos.
6. Complete Google’s verification method and record the verification date.
7. Add staff/vendors at the lowest necessary access level; keep business owner access.

- [ ] Name, phone, hours, website and service area match `docs/01-business-profile.md`.

- [ ] Review monthly and after any change to contact details, hours or service area.
