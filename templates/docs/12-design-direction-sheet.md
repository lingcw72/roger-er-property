## Purpose

Prepare three one-page visual directions for client review before building the website. The client selects one direction before the final site design is created.

## Required content for each direction

1. Direction name, three to five keywords and intended customer impression.
2. Colour palette: primary, accent, text, background and neutral colours with hexadecimal values.
3. Typography: heading, body, label and real business-content samples.
4. Compact hero preview: navigation, headline, supporting copy, calls to action and image treatment.
5. Core components: buttons, service card, review area, project card, FAQ row and quote form fields.
6. Layout treatment: spacing, content width, corners, borders/shadows and image style.
7. Mobile hero and one mobile content card.
8. Photography direction: real/stock images, image mood, people/project focus and before/after treatment.
9. Decision area: best for, strength/trade-off and client choice `A / B / C`.
