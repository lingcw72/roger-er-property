## Minimum lead register

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Lead ID / date received | Identify and time the enquiry |
| Name and contact method | Follow-up |
| Source / campaign | Measure source quality |
| Service / location | Assess fit |
| Status | New / contacted / quotation / qualified / won / lost |
| Value and outcome date | Business reporting |
| GCLID or approved reference | Offline Ads conversion where applicable |

## Process

1. Restrict the register to authorised business staff; do not expose it publicly.
2. Add each confirmed enquiry promptly and assign an owner.
3. Update status using agreed definitions; record the lost reason where useful.
4. Retain and delete personal data according to the approved privacy policy and business practice.
5. Review lead volume, response time, qualified leads and won jobs monthly.
