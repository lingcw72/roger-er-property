# SME Website Project Templates

Copy this `templates/` folder into each new business project, then copy its contents into the matching `docs/`, `runbooks/`, and `assets/legal/` folders. Replace every `[square-bracketed field]` with approved client information.

Never invent business facts, legal terms, tracking IDs, credentials, prices, testimonials, or warranty promises. Leave unknown fields as `Awaiting client input`.

The legal files are controlled-document starters, not publish-ready legal advice. Populate and obtain business/legal approval only after the actual services, data collection, vendors, and commercial terms are known.
